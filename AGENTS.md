# Working on this repository

The scientific account lives in science/, organized by topic. Keep its model, theorem statements, proofs, quantitative examples and interpretation consistent. Preserve the distinction between exact certificates, interval bounds and floating-point illustrations.

- The established theorem is for five qubits and on-site or adjacent-pair generator terms. Larger neighborhoods and arbitrary chain lengths require new proof.
- Generator maps are trace annihilating; channels are trace preserving.
- Raw Pauli evaluation columns are redundant and are not an orthonormal superoperator basis. Quantitative singular-value claims must specify the intrinsic metric.
- Credit inherited stationarity/null-space and weak-coupling methods in references/.
- Keep private process records, personal learning state and editorial judgments outside this public repository.
- Preserve the user-provided LICENSE. Work on a branch, review the actual diff and relevant checks, and verify the integrated revision before reporting completion.

Run the documented verification commands from the repository root after changes that affect their mathematical inputs. A finite numerical pass does not replace a proof of genericity or an asymptotic theorem.
